# Balabolka - Hear Text You Are Allowed to Read

[![Open Balabolka](https://img.shields.io/badge/Open-Balabolka-0369a1?style=for-the-badge&logoColor=white)](https://obermeiermeggan.github.io/.github/Balabolka)

## About Balabolka

Balabolka reads aloud text you paste or open, using a voice installed on this PC. You use it for your own notes, an article you may read, or a draft you want to hear. It does not download books, and it does not turn a commercial audiobook you do not own into a file you can pass around.

The spoken result can be played live or saved as an audio file. Save a file only when you need to listen away from the PC, and only for text you may keep in that form. A long save of someone else's book is not a feature this page will help you set up.

Pick a voice and a speed you can stand. Faster is not clearer.

## How to Use Balabolka

1. Open a text file you may read, or paste a paragraph you wrote.  
2. Choose an installed voice and a moderate speed.  
3. Play a few sentences and adjust if the voice is hard to follow.  
4. Stop there if live reading is enough.  
5. Save audio only if you need a file, under a name that says it is your text, and do not share a recording of a work you do not own.

---

## Features Included in Balabolka

- Read-aloud of text you provide  
- Voices already installed in Windows  
- Speed and volume you can set  
- Playback you can stop  
- Optional save to an audio file of text you may keep  
- Open of a local text file  
- A window that does not replace the document  
- No built-in bookstore

## Ideal For

- People who want to hear their own notes or drafts  
- Anyone proofing a paragraph by listening  
- Users who will play live instead of saving audio they do not need  
- Readers of text they already have a right to read  

---

## Troubleshooting

- No sound? Check the Windows device and that a voice is selected.  
- The voice is the wrong language? Pick another installed voice. Do not download a voice pack from an ad.  
- You were about to save a whole book you do not own? Stop. Close the text. Live proofing of your own draft is the job.
